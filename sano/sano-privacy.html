Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 27, 2026

Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Sano collects, uses, processes, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Processing

Sano is designed to protect your sensitive personal data. We collect information in the following ways:

  • Mental Wellness & Test Data: Any answers you provide to the 20-question assessment, your calculated scores, and progress on your 7-Day Recovery Plans are **stored locally on your device**. We do not transmit, upload, or save your mental wellness scores to external servers.
  • Purchase and Subscription Data: Transactions are handled directly through Apple's App Store via StoreKit 2. We only process anonymous transaction receipts to validate active subscriptions.
  • Device Information: We may collect non-identifiable technical data (device type, operating system version, app version, language preference) to ensure technical stability.

2. Data Storage and iCloud

Your wellness scores and settings are stored locally on your device. They may also be synced automatically to your personal, private Apple iCloud storage if you have iCloud backup enabled on your device. We have no access to your iCloud data.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or share any of your data with third-party marketing networks, analytical companies, or external advertisers.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data is stored locally, deleting the Sano application from your iOS device will automatically erase all local data, scores, and plan progress.

5. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
